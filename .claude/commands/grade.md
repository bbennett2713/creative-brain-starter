description: Grade something the brain made
---
The founder wants to grade or correct something the brain made (an ad, a report, a read). Ask what it is if it isn't clear, take their grade and their why, and turn every why into a one-line rule in the right brain/ file (off-limits.md, house-rules.md, what-worked.md or account-story.md), with where it came from. Show them the new lines. Never make them say it twice.
