# Checkpoint 3: Outside reading

**The idea.** Your account can only teach the brain what you've already tried. The people you trust in advertising have tested things you haven't. Send the brain to read them, and ask it to read your account through their eyes.

---

## Step 1: The reading list (into `brain/reading-list.md`)

- **The founder's move:** "Whose advice about ads do you actually trust? Anyone: a creator, a newsletter, a podcast, an agency, a brand you admire." Two or three names is plenty.
- If they have nobody, suggest 3 to 5 well-known voices on ad creative for their kind of business, say in one line why each, and let them pick. If they say "just do it", pick the two you can actually read and say why in one line each.
- For each person, find **where they post in a form you can read**: newsletters with a public archive (Substack and beehiiv usually are), blogs, YouTube titles and descriptions (the channel's RSS feed, youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=...), podcasts (show notes). YouTube transcripts, LinkedIn and X are usually blocked; say so, and say how thin the skim is because of it. The easy fallback: they forward or paste posts into `reading/`, or subscribe to a newsletter and drop issues there.

## Step 2: The first skim

Read what you can reach (the last month or so), then write a short skim at the top of `brain/reading-list.md`:

- **What are they saying?** Recurring themes across the people, not one post.
- **Every ad format they suggest.** Keep a running list with who suggested it and why it works.
- **How would they read my account?** Apply their lens to `brain/account-story.md` (or to the product and reviews if there are no results yet).
- **What are they doing that we're not?**
- **What have they tested that we haven't?** This is the big one. Each answer is a candidate lane for checkpoint 6.

Cite every point to its post (link or file). Never put words in an expert's mouth.

## Step 3: The founder decides

Ask: "Which one of these would you actually try?" Write their pick and their reason down. It goes to the roadmap as a candidate lane.

- **Hand back:** the skim, plus "the one idea from outside your account I'd test first, and why."

**Done when:** `brain/reading-list.md` names at least one source and has a first skim, or says `Not now`.

**Coming back later:** a weekly skim can run on its own (checkpoint 4). When a new expert or format comes up, add it here.
