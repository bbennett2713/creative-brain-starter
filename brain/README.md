# brain/

The part that makes your ads get better every batch, not just more numerous. Claude reads all of it before every plan and every batch. You can edit any of it by hand.

- `status.md`: where you are on the road, and your decisions.
- `house-rules.md`: how to read ads and make ads. Grows every time you correct something.
- `off-limits.md`: what we never say or show.
- `account-story.md`: the story of your ad account, corrected by you.
- `reading-list.md`: the people you trust and what they're saying.
- `assignments.md`: check-ins that run on their own, and your grades on them.
- `roadmap.md`: the lanes being tested and why.
- `what-worked.md`: winners and why they won.
