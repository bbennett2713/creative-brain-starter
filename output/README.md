# output/

Your ads land here.

- `brand-card-1080x1080.png`: your brand on one page (syllabus day).
- `lab/`: the small graded tests from the lab project, round by round, and `grades.md`.
- `batch-01/`, `batch-02/`...: one folder per experiment. Each has `plan.md` (the experiment card), the ads as square (1080x1080) and tall (1080x1350) PNGs, `batch.md` (what each ad is and where its words came from) and `scores.md`.
